# Readings.nutype

The shape of `readings.nuon`, the readings assembler's record
(`nu/mod/kernel/timing/readings`'s main): the strategy, the checks bound
to their landed runs, the calibration's state, eps, and qualifying
frames, the freeze's roles, the runs, each an fps run, probed or not, or a
walk run with its stamps off or on, every one with its contract, the shim
and TIMING controls, the host limits, the spec's path, and the
assembler's identity. `calibration.qualifying` is keyed by the matrix's
calibration targets and names those `nu/nuon/kernel/timing/matrix.nuon`
declares, 1ka1 and 1kb1. A landing holds a readings record to it
(`runner record outputs`).

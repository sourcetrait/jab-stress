# Decision.nutype

The shape of `decision.nuon`, the timing selector's decision
(`nu/mod/kernel/timing`'s main over `decide`): the strategy, the declared
hosts, the validation's refusals and shortfalls, the declared and the
recomputed calibration, P2's eligibility with its checks, slots, and why,
the rows of each predicate, the regression, the presentation, and walk's,
the noise gate, the blockers, the outcome, its scope, the box's own
outcome, the contract, the readings' and the matrix's SHA-256, and the
selector's identity. The declared calibration's `qualifying` is keyed by
the matrix's calibration targets, which the grammar can state only as
fields: it names the targets `nu/nuon/kernel/timing/matrix.nuon` declares,
1ka1 and 1kb1, so a target added there is added here. A landing holds a
decision to it (`runner record outputs`).

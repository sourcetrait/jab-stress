# Qualification.nutype

The shape of `qualification.nuon`, a target's qualification record
(`nu/mod/fierce/qualification/record`'s main; check's main writes one as
`check/qualification.nuon` from the same `record-of`): the target, when
it was written, the readers' identity, the candidate, the spec, the
bench, the conditions, the hosts, the watch, the RAM, the runs, the
cases, the diagnostics, the ledger, and the verdicts. `candidate.builds`
is keyed by the bench files' named builds and names those jab's
`kit/fierce/bench/qual_1ka1.nuon` and `qual_1kb1.nuon` give, service and
debug. The watch, and each run's identity, measurement, CPU, and audio,
are copies of the bench's own records, stated as their writers write them
today, so a record over a bench older than those writers' changes holds
the shapes they wrote then. A landing holds a target's record to it
(`runner record outputs`); the check's record, a derivation over the kept
bench, is kept as written.

# Reading.nutype

The shape of `reading.nuon`, footprint's reading
(`nu/mod/fierce/footprint/verdicts`' read): each view with its tallies,
its floor, its population, its verdicts by the declared and the secant
reference, its no-new-departure check, its blocks, and its pictures or
null; the refused and the missing poses; and the whole reading's
verdicts. A landing holds a reading to it (`runner record outputs`).

# moves.nuon

The moves a manifest taken before them is read across: `paths`, a
program's path from the checkout's root moved whole with everything
under it, and `names`, a program whose manifest's name changed, its
files named by it, `<name>.jab`, `<name>.elf`, and `<name>.romfs`, taking
the new name. KitFierce's move is both: `game/fps` to `kit/fierce`, its
fixture programs with it, and the game's name `fps` to `fierce`.

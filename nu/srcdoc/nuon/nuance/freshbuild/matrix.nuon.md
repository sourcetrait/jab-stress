# matrix.nuon

Every tuple jab's source builds, as a survey just before the manifest
found them: the `--set` literals in code and docs, the runners', the
benches', and the workbench builders' sets, the exports' symbols, and
walk's launch table. A row is a program, `*` for the whole workspace, or
`kernel` for the kernel alone, with the tuples it is built at.

- `*`: release and DEBUG, as `build` and the suite build them.
- kit/fierce: COUNT, CENSUS, COUNT and HANDOFF, HANDOFF, OWNER, and STATS
  under DEBUG; LEVELS, the level comparison's images; RELEASE, the pool
  export's build; SERVICE, the qualification benches' `service` build, and
  SERVICE under DEBUG, a test's `--set service`; IZ_TEST with OWNER and with
  LEVELS under DEBUG, the footprint builder's clamp images. STATS and
  HANDOFF condition no assembly; their builds differ by the defined symbol
  alone.
- kit/fierce/test/fillrate: VECTOR, release and under DEBUG.
- example/walk: launches.nu's six CADENCE_TEST tuples, the walk export's
  four CADENCE tuples, and PREPARE under DEBUG.
- example/bounce, wasd, and pad: PREPARE under DEBUG.

A tuple the source builds that this file lacks is a defect of the matrix,
found when the cutover's builds run.

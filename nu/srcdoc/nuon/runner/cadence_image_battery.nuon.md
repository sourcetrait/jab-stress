# cadence_image_battery.nuon

Two builds at one cadence, one by its image, compared. The long engine's body
alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready cadence_image_battery`,
then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work cadence_image_battery` with the
progress check and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose bin both
run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps`, `base`, `batches`
each batch's label and image, empty for the fresh build, `fields`.

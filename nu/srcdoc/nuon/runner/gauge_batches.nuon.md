# gauge_batches.nuon

Gauge batches in a declared order, each under its own environment. The long
engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
gauge_batches`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work gauge_batches`
with the progress check, the batches' logs counted, and the bounds, `stress`
the workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps` fierce's directory in
jab, `base` the battery's new directory, `batches` each batch's `label`, its
`words` after `gauge run`, and its `env`, a table of `name` and `value`, and
`max_quiet_min` the quiet limit in minutes.

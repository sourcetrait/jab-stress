# scaling_batteries.nuon

GfxScaling's selections on three, four, and eight harts, settled and factory.
The long engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
scaling_batteries`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work
scaling_batteries` with the progress check and the bounds, `stress` the
workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps`, `gauge`, `new` the
new image, `base62` the base image, `settled` the settled route's file.

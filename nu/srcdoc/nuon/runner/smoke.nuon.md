# smoke.nuon

fierce's development smoke by its bin. The long engine's body alone: the harness
made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready smoke`, then its work launched as
`nuance.nu runner work smoke` with the progress check and the bounds, `stress`
the workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `nuance` fierce's
`nu/bin/nuance.nu`, `seeds` comma separated, `seconds` each seed's play,
`smoke_dir` where the smoke writes, `log` the log's file.

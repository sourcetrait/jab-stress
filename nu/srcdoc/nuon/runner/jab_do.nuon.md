# jab_do.nuon

A root driver's verb in the jab checkout under an environment, logged. The long
engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready jab_do`,
then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work jab_do` with the progress check
and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `jab` the checkout, `bin`
`do` or `nuance`, the root's driver, `verb` and `words` its command, `env` a
table of `name` and `value` set for it, `log` the log's file, `max_quiet_min`
the engine's quiet bound in minutes.

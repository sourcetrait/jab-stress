# jab_programs.nuon

Several programs' tests through jab's driver in turn. The long engine's body
alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready jab_programs`, then its
work launched as `nuance.nu runner work jab_programs` with the progress check
and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `jab`, `paths` the
programs' paths, `log` the log's file.

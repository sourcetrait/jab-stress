# jab_probes.nuon

A series of sdl probes of one program in a declared order. The long engine's
definition alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
jab_probes`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work jab_probes` with
the progress check, the runs' records counted, and the bounds, `stress` the
workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its data past `stress` and `label`: `jab` the jab checkout,
`program` the program's path there, `probe_dir` the probe's directory in the
target, `base` the series' new directory, `runs` each run's `label` and its
`words` after `sdl <program>`, and `max_quiet_min` the quiet limit in minutes.

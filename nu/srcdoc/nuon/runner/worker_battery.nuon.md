# worker_battery.nuon

Gauge batches at each one's workers and grain, compared by field. The long
engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
worker_battery`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work
worker_battery` with the progress check and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's
checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: as two_build_battery's,
each batch with its `workers` and `grain`, and `cpu` whether the runs record
their CPU.

# walk_battery.nuon

walk's CADENCE runs in a declared order, each batch a condition's image and
kernel. The long engine's definition alone: the harness made ready by
`nuance.nu runner ready walk_battery`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu
runner work walk_battery` with the progress check, the batches' logs and the
runs' directories counted, and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout,
whose bin both run.

Its data past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `jab` the jab checkout,
`base` the battery's new directory, `batches` each batch's `label`, its walk
CADENCE `image`, its `kernel`, its `spec`, and its `runs`, and `max_quiet_min`
the quiet limit in minutes. Its nonce is empty until its definition first runs.

# two_build_battery.nuon

A gauge battery of two builds, batches alternated, compared by field. The long
engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
two_build_battery`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work
two_build_battery` with the progress check and the bounds, `stress` the
workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps`, `base`, `batches`
each label and image, `runs`, `cadence`, `route`, `fields`, `log_glob` the logs
the check counts.

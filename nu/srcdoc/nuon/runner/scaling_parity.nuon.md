# scaling_parity.nuon

Worker parity and fault steps on one kernel, each step's verdict read. The long
engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
scaling_parity`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work
scaling_parity` with the progress check and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's
checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `nuance` the parity bin,
`nu/bin/nuance/fierce/workerparity.nu`, `kernel`, `base` the parity's new
directory, `steps` each step's name, `recipe` `parity` or `fault`, image, and
flags.

# grain_battery.nuon

Forced W 3 at 16 and 32 rows on four and eight harts, settled and factory. The
long engine's body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready
grain_battery`, then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work grain_battery`
with the progress check and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose
bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps`, `gauge` the
batteries' parent directory, `image` the image every batch runs, `settled` the
settled route's file.

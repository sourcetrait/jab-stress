# tilecensus_reading.nuon

The tile census read over a battery's batches. The long engine's body alone: the
harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready tilecensus_reading`, then its work
launched as `nuance.nu runner work tilecensus_reading` with the progress check
and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose bin both run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `nuance` the tile census
bin, `nu/bin/nuance/fierce/tilecensus.nu`, `base` a battery's directory,
`labels` its batches.

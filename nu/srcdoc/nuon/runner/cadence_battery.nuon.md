# cadence_battery.nuon

Gauge batches at their cadences, compared with a baseline. The long engine's
body alone: the harness made ready by `nuance.nu runner ready cadence_battery`,
then its work launched as `nuance.nu runner work cadence_battery` with the
progress check and the bounds, `stress` the workbench's checkout, whose bin both
run.

Its arguments past `stress`, `label`, and `mcp_nom`: `fps` fierce's directory in
jab, `base` the battery's new directory, `batches` each batch's label and
cadence, `baseline` gauge.nuon files compared beside them, `fields` the fields
compared.

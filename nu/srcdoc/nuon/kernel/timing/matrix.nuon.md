# matrix.nuon

JointTiming's comparison as declared (ComparisonMatrix), written from its
steps 2 to 4 and never discovered from the runs; the selector holds every
readings record to it before any rule.

- `hosts`: the box always, with its targets, 1KA1 and 1KB1, and the battery
  each of its predicates decides by: the guest side by `guest`, the display
  side by `display`, the second workload by `walk`. TheUser's host joins by
  name once his bench, step 5, joins the decision, deciding the guest side
  by his display battery's unprobed runs.
- `legs`: the factory route's legs, every one an fps run's figures cover.
- `calibration`: eps's source, the box's guest battery's P0 runs, both
  batches, their captures read anew.
- `batteries`: each a host's, its workload, its backend (headless,
  offscreen, window), its runs a batch, its runs a condition, its seeds a
  batch in run order, its conditions, and its batches in run order, a
  condition's name each. A condition's kernel, loop order, TIMING, probe,
  and image role, the role the freeze names its export by; its kernel role
  the kernel's.
  - `guest`, step 2: fps release, headless and unprobed, P0 and P2 in the
    order P0, P2, P2, P0, seeds 1 to 3, six runs a condition.
  - `display`, step 3: fps in offscreen SDL, P0 and P2 probed and unprobed,
    in the order P0 probed, P2 probed, P0, P2, P2, P0, P2 probed, P0 probed,
    seeds 1 to 3.
  - `walk`, step 4: walk's CADENCE build, the on conditions in arm order (P0
    await, P2 await, P2 prepare), the off conditions in the same order (P0
    await, P2 prepare), the off reversed, the on reversed, three runs a batch
    at the build's fixed seed, CADENCE_SEED 0x2545f4914f6cdd1d.

fps's order is `prepare`: at cadence 1 the game draws, waits only while
early, then flips.

# mod.nu

The gauge_batches runner's code, reached by its descriptor through the
workbench bin's `runner` verbs, or run whole by `do.nu bench gauge_batches
<args>`. A battery of fierce's gauge batches in a declared order, each with the
words and the environment of its own, so the arms of a control differ by what
the battery names alone: the shim, the window, the sound backend.

## def ready

Before the launch: refuses a `base` that exists or a label that two batches
share, then makes `base` and writes the batches as declared to
`<base>/batches.nuon`, the battery's own record of what it was asked to run.

## def main

The work: each batch in its order, fierce's `nu/bin/nuance.nu gauge run` with
the batch's words, `--out <base>/<label>`, and `--label <label>`, run from
fierce's directory under the batch's environment, a table of `name` and
`value`; its stdout then its stderr saved as `<base>/<label>.log`. Nothing is
built: a batch names its image and kernel in its words, a frozen export's. A
batch that exits other than 0 raises its label, its exit, and its log's last
six lines, the batches after it not run. Returns the labels done.

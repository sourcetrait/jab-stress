# disks/mod.nu

A bench's disk copies made again from today's content and held to the bench's
records, the removal gate's check of copies kept as their SHA-256: each step
plays one disk, a romfs of the map's compiled tree, whose tree's digest each
run's identity records.

## const GAME

The game in jab, checked out beside the workbench, whose map bin compiles a
map's tree.

## const DISKS

The result's file in `out`, `disks.nuon`.

## def assets-of

A step's one asset tree as its runs' identities record it: the map, the
tree's digest, and the content beside the program's directory, where the
bench's checkout compiled it. A step with no run's identity, or whose runs
record more than one tree, is named.

## def made-again

An asset tree compiled from today's content into `dir` through the map bin,
its digest (gauge's `identity tree-digest`) held to the recorded one; one of
another digest rooted as the bench's checkout compiled it, its tile.nuon's
sources under that content (tilesources), and its digest taken again. The
disk made over the tree with the volume `fps`, and its SHA-256.

## def main

Each disk copy the inventory lists, a `.romfs`, by its step: the step's
asset tree, each distinct tree compiled once into `out/assets_<n>`, the copy
held when its tree's digest is the identities' and its disk's SHA-256 the
inventory's. `disks.nuon` holds the bench, the inventory, each tree's digests
compiled and rooted, and each copy's verdict; each tree's verdict and each
copy not held printed, the count last. A copy not held raises.

# mod.nu

AdmissionMeasure's reading (FrameCeiling's PoolCore): each batch under `base`
named in `labels`, its report's frames at schema 5, 6, or 7, every run's.
Refused before any verdict, every run named (PoolReaderStanding), unless every
run is complete, valid, and seeded at one of SCHEMAS. Per batch: the runs and
frames; the requests admitted, dropped at a full open ring, and filtered by the
repeat filter; the frames dropping any and the most a frame dropped and
admitted; the duplicates among the processed requests, each boundary's source
batch's admitted less its unprocessed less its merged, read from the boundary's
own tile record, the overlap across contexts among them; the unprocessed, whose
duplicates stay unknown; the greatest open ring's fill and requesting surfaces.
The verdict: TILE_RING and TILE_GUARANTEE stand at the census's provisional
sizes only when no frame of any batch dropped a request. Written to `out` as
nuon and printed. Its bin: `nu/bin/nuance/fierce/admission.nu <base> <out>
<label>...`.

## const SCHEMAS

the schemas whose pool records the reading takes: 5, and 6 and 7, the
same records beside a frame's harts record and, at 7, its receipt

## def total-of

A list's sum and greatest, its nulls dropped: 0 and null for an empty
one, which math's own refuse.

## def load

Each batch's report under `base`, by label.

## def standing-of

The runs not complete, valid, and seeded at one of SCHEMAS, each named.

## def reading-of

The reading of loaded batches whose runs all stand.

## def read-or-refuse

The reading, or the refusal naming every run that does not stand.

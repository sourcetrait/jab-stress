# mod.nu

The release tier's tile eviction fixtures (TileEviction) on a debug image of
jab's fps and the still disk, each a launch read from its records (gauge
measure) and its tile dumps' keys (the tiles bin's `keys`, in a nu of its own),
asserting as the fps campaign specifies them:

- cycle, EvictionCycle: the ordinary budget, real lumels, the default workers, a
cap of CAP slots under the spawn view and the bay alternated, each held to its
settle and dumped; the last spawn's picture and its reference's each bound by
its stamp to a validated, settled frame (picture-refusals), and the two equal
outside the stamp.

- rebuild, RebuildBound: the same cycle under the lift with a quota of
REBUILD_TILES whole tiles a boundary and the stale mode, so construction is
counted work and every phase's rebuild publishes at exactly its frames.

- held, HeldPartial: the spawn's construction held partial by the lift, a quota,
and ONCE through the screen and E, its picture refused as unsettled and for
nothing else.

- reuse, SlotReuse: every lumel full bright, W 2 in fixed bands with worker 2
held until the join releases it; the spawn's return from the yard under a cap,
the stale mode, and ONCE, one construction leaving a tile BUILDING in a slot a
yard tile left; both pictures' evidence anchored at the spawn placement the
guest recorded, then the picture the bright view held off its tiles outside the
stamp, its partial tile's rows read apart.

Its bin: `nu/bin/nuance/fierce/stress/eviction.nu <kernel> <image> <disk> <out>
[fixture...]`, every fixture by default.

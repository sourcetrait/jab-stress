# mod.nu

The cadence fixtures alone on a debug image of jab's fps: suite/clock's
cadence-holds, CadenceSchedule and MotionByTime at each cadence a run may ask,
the program's one, each a launch measured from its records, the fixtures
building their own Render One and still Render Zero disks under `out`. Prints
`cadence: ok` once every fixture holds.

`--set` names the build's symbols as the fixtures' launches take them, `DEBUG`
unless given, the image and kernel built under the same symbols, as `./do
build kit/fierce --set debug` builds them. The fixtures hold each presented
flip to the kernel's frame rule and K at its FRAME_EPS (`rule-holds`).

Its bin: `nu/bin/nuance/fierce/stress/cadence.nu <kernel> <image> <out>
[--set DEBUG]`.

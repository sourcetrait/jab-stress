# mod.nu

TileEviction's planning probe: the still copy's spawn view (A) and the same eye
turned about (B), placed in turn under a slot cap, every settle dumped (D, every
surface), the tiles' keys, slots, generations, and texel signatures read back
with the pool's counters a frame. Mode building dumps the spawn view's settle
after a cold start under the ordinary budget in pairs, a plain D and one with
byte 8 at the same moment, read by the tiles bin's keys in a nu of its own. Its
bin: `nu/bin/nuance/fierce/probe/eviction.nu <kernel> <image> <disk> <out>
<mode> [slots]`.

## const QUOTA_WHOLE

the quota mode's: 32 whole tiles a boundary, and 32 tiles and 8 rows

## def dump-headers

every dump's tile headers with a signature of their texels

## def main

- the spawn's view is A from the start, so its settle follows the
  cold start, about 17 ms a frame
- whole tiles to the settle; then one construction a phase under
  ONCE, held for its dump: 32 tiles and 8 rows from a cold start,
  32 tiles from that partial, 32 tiles and 8 rows from the next
- the dumps alternate plain and asked, in the order sent; a pair sent
  at one moment can still fall to two frames

# mod.nu

A pair run's reading (the eviction probe's `pair` mode): each settle's dump
against the two views' key sets, each phase's builds, evictions, rebuilt keys,
and frames to settle, the tiles held in place or moved, every key's texels
across the dumps, the slots' generations, and the resets and memory over the
run. `sets` is a `views` run's nuon, whose dumps name each view's keys at first
sight; `a` and `b` index its views.

# mod.nu

The tile pool's knobs probed on the game's debug build: the spawn view under one
mode's O frame, `rotation`, `lifted`, `ordinary`, or `stale`, the pool's passes
traced on the UART; each run's rows, configurations, and traced passes kept in
probe.nuon, and a stale run's screen held to the held capture's rows. Its bin:
`nu/bin/nuance/fierce/probe/knobs.nu <mode> <out> [--seconds N]`.
